# Code Of Conduct

We want this project to be useful, careful, and welcoming. Contributors are expected to treat each other with respect, especially when reviewing safety-sensitive work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, direct, and specific.
- Assume good intent, but verify safety claims with evidence.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project context.
- Keep disagreement focused on the design, data, and risks.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety boundaries and release gates.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Dismissing safety concerns without evidence.
- Pressuring people to perform unsafe live-appliance testing.
- Publishing instructions that bypass heater safety systems.
- Sharing private field photos, serial numbers, addresses, or contact information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or delete comments; close issues or pull requests; or block accounts that violate this code. Serious safety concerns may be escalated by moving discussion out of public threads until the risk is understood.

Report conduct issues privately to the project maintainers through GitHub.
